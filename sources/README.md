# sources/

**Your documents. The agent reads them and never edits them.**

Put in here whatever describes your working life:

- Your CV, in whatever state it is in. It does not need tidying first — a messy one is more useful,
  because the gaps are informative.
- A LinkedIn data export, or a copy-paste of your profile. **Worth having even if you think it duplicates
  the CV** — the two disagree more often than people expect, and every disagreement is a finding.
- Job specs you are considering.
- Performance reviews, strengths assessments, 360 feedback, anything with an outside view of you.
- Old project documents you had a hand in.

Everything in this folder is **gitignored**. It stays on your machine.

## One thing worth knowing

The most valuable material in your career wiki will not come from these files. It comes from the
interview. These documents exist to give the agent enough context to ask good questions.
