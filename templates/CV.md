🟡 **Ask for a LinkedIn export as well as the CV.** The two disagree surprisingly often — dates, titles,
whole roles missing — **and each disagreement is a finding, not a nuisance.** LinkedIn is usually the more
granular of the two. Record which one you followed and why.

## What each role actually involved

**One short section per role**, in the user's own words where you have them.

🔴 **Mark every claim drawn from a document as unverified** — no `verified` key until the user confirms it
in conversation. **Most of it turns out to be slightly wrong**, and a confident sentence nobody has checked
is the thing that ends up in front of a recruiter.

🔴 **A number without a source is not a fact.** If the CV says *"reduced costs by 30%"* and nothing says how
it was measured, record the claim AND that it is unsourced. **Never resolve it by adding the figure to the
wiki** — that launders an invention into a source.
